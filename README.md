# a little place for you

> *“made with attention, not perfection.”*

A quiet, mobile-first interactive experience made by Samir for Intie.

---

### Features & Spaces
- **The Quiet Room**: Central spatial metaphor linking desk, warm lamp, notebook, water glass, and night garden.
- **Things I Know About You**: 6 interactive volumes capturing unprompted research rabbit holes, midnight questions, and quiet observations.
- **The Racing Brain**: Interactive notebook experience for midnight overthinking, bringing calm and settling: *“Not every thought needs an answer tonight.”*
- **Personal Field Guide**: Notes on quiet care, listening, and being steady.
- **When She Isn’t Feeling Okay**: Clear, calm safety notes (including critical anesthesia allergy note), migraine care, and tender reassurance.
- **Feed Her Properly**: Affectionate kitchen notes celebrating ramen and legendary spice tolerance, with firm boundaries on small fish.
- **Water Garden**: Botanic illustrations of her favorite blooms — *Shapla* (water lily) and *Padma* (lotus).
- **Weekly Rhythm**: Sunday, Tuesday, Thursday study schedule.
- **Memories & Horizons**: Relationship timeline (from September 29, 2026), September 26 interactive birthday candle, and keepsake box.
- **For You**: The quietest corner of the room, resting on textured paper.

---

### Tech & Performance
- **100% Static & Client-Side**: Zero servers, zero databases, zero external API keys.
- **GitHub Pages Ready**: Builds with relative paths (`./assets/`) for seamless hosting at root or repository subpaths.
- **Procedural Sound**: Procedural Web Audio API rain and gentle droplet synthesis (0KB audio downloads, default OFF).
- **Adaptive Performance**: Supports high, balanced, and battery-saver quality modes; respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Privacy First**: No private addresses, phone numbers, emails, or credentials in any file.
